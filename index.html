Mission

The mission of the American Asian Caribbean Group (AACG) is to strengthen collaboration between America, Asia, and the Caribbean by promoting economic cooperation, cultural exchange, leadership development, and strategic partnerships.

AACG seeks to empower entrepreneurs, professionals, institutions, and community leaders by creating opportunities for innovation, collaboration, and shared growth across regions.

“Strengthening collaboration between America, Asia, and the Caribbean through economic cooperation, culture, and shared growth.”
— AACG Mission
