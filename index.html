American Asian Caribbean Group, Inc.

Connecting America, Asia, and the Caribbean through leadership, partnerships, and cultural exchange.

The American Asian Caribbean Group (AACG) is a platform that fosters collaboration, economic partnerships, cultural exchange, and leadership initiatives between communities and institutions across the United States, Asia, and the Caribbean.

Learn More Upcoming Events

Our Mission

The mission of the American Asian Caribbean Group (AACG) is to build meaningful bridges between America, Asia, and the Caribbean by promoting economic cooperation, cultural exchange, leadership development, and strategic partnerships.

AACG seeks to empower entrepreneurs, professionals, institutions, and community leaders by creating opportunities for collaboration, innovation, and shared growth across regions.

“Building meaningful bridges between three dynamic regions through cooperation, culture, and shared growth.”
— AACG Vision
